What this shows

A quality inspector walks the station with the single-line diagram in hand and compares every part with what was actually installed. Each difference is marked in red on the drawing (a red-line) and, when it affects safety or protection, raised as a non-conformance report (NCR) for the contractor to fix.

Design numbers

630 kVA, 20/0.4 kV, uk 6 %. Rated current 18.2 A on the HV side and 909 A on the LV side, so a 1000 A incoming breaker. Prospective short-circuit current at the LV busbar about 15.2 kA (stiff grid), which every breaker on the board must be able to break.
